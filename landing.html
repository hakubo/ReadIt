Read it!

Select text on any page and listen to it with AI voice

Install for Chrome

How it works

1

Select text

Highlight any text on any webpage

2

Click "Read"

A button appears next to your selection

3

Listen

Natural AI voice reads the text aloud

Why Read it!?

🔒

100% Private

Your text never leaves your browser. No servers, no APIs, no tracking.

💻

Runs Locally

The AI model runs entirely on your device using WebGPU or WASM.

🆓

Free & Open Source

No subscriptions, no limits, no accounts required.

🎙️

Natural Voices

Multiple high-quality voices across languages, powered by Kokoro TTS.

System requirements

  • Chrome 113+ with WebGPU support (or any Chromium-based browser)
  • ~500 MB RAM for the TTS model
  • Works best with a dedicated GPU, but CPU mode is also supported
