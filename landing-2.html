Why I built this

Every TTS tool I found had the same problem: it either sounded robotic, required a monthly subscription, or shipped my text off to some server for processing. I just wanted to listen to articles while doing other things. I didn't want to create an account for that. I definitely didn't want to pay $10/month for it.

So I built unmute.page. It runs a real AI voice model directly in the browser — no cloud, no middleman. It works out of the box but it's also configurable for people who want control: different voices per website, speed adjustments, sentence highlighting, the works.

The extension is free and open source. If it saves you time or makes your day a little better, consider buying me a coffee.
