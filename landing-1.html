Turn any webpage into something you can listen to

A Chrome extension that reads web pages aloud with natural AI voices. Free, private, and entirely local.

Add to Chrome View source

Your text never leaves your browser. Voice generation happens 100% on your device. No servers, no APIs, no data collection. Your reading habits are yours alone.

How it works

  1. Select text on any page

    Highlight a paragraph, an article, or an entire page. A small "Read" button appears next to your selection.

  2. Click "Read" and listen

    A floating player appears. The AI voice starts reading immediately while generating the rest in the background.

  3. Customize to your taste

    Choose from 54 natural voices, adjust speed from 0.6x to 2x, enable sentence highlighting, and save per-site preferences.

What makes it different

Private by design

The entire TTS model runs inside your browser using ONNX and WebAssembly. Nothing is ever sent to the internet. Not your text, not your usage data, nothing.

No subscriptions

Install it once, use it forever. No accounts, no trial periods, no premium tiers. Every feature is available to everyone.

54 natural voices

Powered by Kokoro TTS, a high-quality neural voice model. Voices are downloaded once and cached locally for instant access.

Deeply configurable

Per-site voice preferences, adjustable speed, sentence highlighting with color options, content area selectors, and text replacement rules.

Works offline

After the initial model download (~326 MB), everything works without an internet connection. Voices are cached locally.

Open source

The full source code is available on GitHub. Inspect it, modify it, contribute to it. You own your tools.

Why I built this

I kept running into the same problem: every text-to-speech tool I tried either sounded terrible, required a monthly subscription, or sent my text to some server I didn't control. I didn't want to pay $10/month just to have articles read to me. I didn't want to wonder who was reading my data on the other end.

So I built what I wanted: something that just works. Select text, click a button, listen. No sign-up, no credit card, no cloud dependency. The AI model runs entirely in your browser, which means your text stays on your machine.

But I also wanted it to be configurable for people who care about the details. Different voices for different sites. Speed adjustments. Sentence highlighting so you can follow along. Per-domain settings so your preferences stick.

This extension is free and will stay free. If you find it useful, a coffee is always appreciated.
